May 2026
DigiGuard Modular wearable for adaptive finger tremor stabilization
Chief Manufacturing Officer
  • Co-developed a wearable device to mechanically suppress finger tremors for patients with neurodegenerative conditions such as ALS and Parkinson's disease.
  • Combined an Arduino, a 360° servo motor, a custom 3D-printed brace, and a finger-mounted accelerometer to track and dampen tremor oscillations.
  • Directed four design cycles, moving from fixed mechanical springs to an automated cord-and-pulley system that reduced false activation.
  • Statistically validated device engagement across 1,400+ movement measurements using Welch's t-test.
  • Result: 84% average reduction in finger tremor magnitude during automated cycles.

Fusion-Based Driver State Monitoring Independent research
  • Built a real-time driver fatigue monitor on a Raspberry Pi, fusing an ArduCam, a force-sensing resistor seat array, and an accelerometer.
  • Trained a custom YOLOv8 model for eye-closure detection and an algorithm to fuse visual and physical signals.
  • Validated slumping and head-tilt markers against lighting and eyewear conditions using t-tests and Fisher's exact tests (p < 0.01).
  • Result: 0.98 AUC and a 473.88 ms response time, about 3x faster than human perception. Awarded 2nd place at the Massachusetts Science & Engineering Fair.
