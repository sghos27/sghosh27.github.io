StabiliQ

A proposed tremor-stabilizing wearable designed around magnetorheological (MR) fluid, which would harden when sensors detect a tremor and relax afterward, so joints move freely the rest of the time. We pitched the design at WPI’s i3 Lab Demo Day and plan to build it next.

The problem

Hand and finger tremors from Parkinson’s, MS, ALS, and essential tremor interfere with everyday tasks, and existing devices mostly target only major tremors.

How it will work

IMU sensors will read motion to detect tremors, and MR fluid dampers at the wrist, elbow, and fingers will stiffen in response, so suppression is adaptive and location-aware. Unlike mechanical dampening, the joints stay free otherwise.

What’s next

We will be filing a provisional patent and building the first prototype. The plan also includes modular supports that can be repaired or upgraded individually, a clinical dashboard for long-term tremor monitoring, and sales to patients and clinics with insurance and distributor partnerships.
